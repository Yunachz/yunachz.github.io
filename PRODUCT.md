## Product Purpose

A single-page, high-craft, bilingual (Indonesian/English) technical portfolio and digital curriculum vitae for **Muhammad Fajar Sodik**, an undergraduate Electrical Engineering student at Universitas Pembangunan Nasional Veteran Jakarta. The product delivers an authentic, grounded presentation of his practical experience in electrical power installations, control systems, IoT prototypes, and software tools without synthetic inflation or AI tropes.

## Positioning

**Grounded, verifiable engineering craft over boilerplate portfolio claims.** Unlike generic web-developer templates with inflated metrics and AI buzzwords, this portfolio reflects real-world engineering discipline: hands-on hotel electrical maintenance (ASTON Kemayoran), physical wiring and PLC control systems, circuit analysis, microcontrollers, and active student organization participation. All achievements, dates, and project scopes are strictly factual.

## Operating Context

- Evaluated primarily on desktop viewports (1440×900 standard review screen) where evaluators examine portfolio sections alongside CV PDFs and code repositories.
- Evaluated on mobile viewports (320–390px) by recruiters and clients scanning links shared via LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or email.
- Users switch between Indonesian (native) and English interfaces depending on whether the evaluation context is domestic or multinational.
- Evaluators expect fast direct access to the downloadable CV (PDF) and real code repositories without nested navigation dead-ends.

## Capabilities and Constraints

### Core Capabilities
1. **Compact Unified Hero:** Combines profile summary, student identity, authentic portrait photograph, primary CTA actions (Explore Projects, View CV), and factual horizontal metric cards (GPA 3,72 / 3.72, Jakarta location, native Indonesian, basic English) within a single desktop viewport (target height 700–820px at 1440×900).
2. **Data-Driven Project Showcase:** Displays 5 audited projects with real category filtering (Control Systems, IoT, Machine Learning, Desktop Apps; no synthetic PLC filter), horizontal looping cards with manual pause/play/arrow controls, touch swipe, and a same-page dialog for technical details and repository links.
3. **Dual-Tab Capabilities ("What I can do" / "Yang bisa saya lakukan"):**
   - **Skills Tab:** 10 audited skills arranged in an interactive horizontal expanding accordion. Collapsed cards display rotated whole-word vertical labels over thematic backgrounds; hover, focus, or tap widens the card and rotates the heading horizontal while revealing the detailed description.
   - **Software Tab:** 7 user-authorized software tools (MATLAB, Arduino IDE, STM32Cube, Altium, Fusion, Proteus, TIA Portal) displayed as translucent Apple glass icon tiles with clean labels.
4. **Chronological Education Timeline:** Two-column desktop layout (Formal education on left, Non-formal training on right) using a connected-dots vertical timeline with the most recent entries at the top, collapsing gracefully on mobile.
5. **Layered Experience Gallery:** All 9 audited organizational, committee, and OJT experiences presented in a manual looping landscape-photo gallery (no autoplay, touch/drag/arrow navigation) with full role, period, summary, and activity details placed directly below the active image.
6. **Six Static Certificate Cards:** Exact canonical certificate titles paired with landscape preview frames and neutral local placeholders where original scans are unavailable (no modals, fake serials, or synthetic verification links).
7. **Tactile Contact Section:** Warm invitation to collaborate ("Let's connect" / "Let's work together"), nested email card with one-click localized clipboard copy and fallback `mailto:`, plus direct links to LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and GitHub.
8. **In-Popup CV Viewer Flow:** Modal begins with an explicit ID/EN language selector, transitioning in-place within the same dialog into a responsive CV preview and revealing the corresponding PDF download action.
9. **Interactive Header Chrome:** Brand "Fajar.", responsive navigation, persistent ID/EN toggle, and single brightness icon driving a circular expanding wave reveal transition in both directions.

## Product Principles

1. **Uncompromised Product Truth:** Every skill, role, project description, and credential reflects audited reality; no synthetic metrics or exaggerated claims.
2. **Tactile Fluidity & Immediate Response:** Interactions follow Apple fluid interface mechanics: instant pointer-down feedback, interruptible spring curves, and 1:1 direct manipulation.
3. **Balanced Viewport Hierarchy:** Desktop viewports keep critical introduction facts above the fold (~700–820px) without cramped density; mobile viewports flow naturally with readable type and generous touch targets (≥44px).
4. **Natural Bilingual Phrasing:** Both Indonesian and English public copy are crafted with native technical precision, avoiding literal translations or machine-generated awkwardness.
5. **Rigorous Accessibility & Resilience:** Full WCAG AA/AAA contrast ratios, keyboard trap prevention, ARIA live states, and complete operational parity under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA contrast standards across both Light and Dark themes.
- Touch target floor of 44×44px for all interactive controls (navigation links, tab buttons, carousel arrows, modal close targets).
- Full keyboard operability (Tab, Shift+Tab, Arrow keys, Escape) across the horizontal accordion, carousel galleries, and nested modal dialogs.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance: immediate transitions, static theme switches, disabled automatic looping, and zero jarring vestibular motion.
- Focus containment and inert background handling for all dialog overlays.
